Understanding personal safety

Personal safety includes feeling secure in your home, relationships, and surroundings.

Describe what would feel safer

Safety can depend on where you are, who is around, and whether you have control over how you leave or ask for help.

Consider what support you can reach privately

A trusted person or an advocate may help you think through options. Choose a way to make contact that feels safe for your situation.
