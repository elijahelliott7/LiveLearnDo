Understanding personal goals

Personal goals help you decide what matters to you and choose a next step you can work toward.

Start with what matters to you

A goal can help you choose where to put your time. Consider whether it reflects something you want or a deadline you need to meet.

Pick a step you can revisit

Choose one action and decide when to check whether it helped. You can adjust the goal as you learn what is realistic.
