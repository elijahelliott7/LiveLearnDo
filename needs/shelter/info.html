Understanding shelter

Shelter means having a safe place to stay and enough stability to plan beyond tonight.

Consider both tonight and the next few weeks

Someone can have a place to sleep tonight and still face uncertainty about where to stay next. Timing helps distinguish urgent shelter from longer-term housing needs.

Name your practical requirements

Consider who needs to stay with you, accessibility, transportation, pets, and whether you need help staying in your current home.
