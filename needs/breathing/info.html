Understanding breathing

Breathing is a basic physical need. The air around you, your surroundings, and how comfortable you feel breathing can all affect daily life.

Notice the surroundings

Think about when the air feels comfortable and when it does not. The room, activity, or time of day can help you describe what you are noticing.

Choose a starting point

If the concern is tied to a building, identify who manages the space. If it follows you across settings, write down what you notice so you can explain it when seeking support.
