Understanding self-worth

Self-worth is the sense that you matter. It can shape how comfortable you feel asking for help and setting boundaries.

Notice what affects how you see yourself

Feedback, stress, and the way people treat you can influence whether you feel valued and comfortable expressing your needs.

Look for environments that respect your boundaries

Consider who listens without dismissing you and where you can participate at your own pace. Those are useful qualities to look for when seeking support.
