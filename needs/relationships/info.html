Understanding relationships

Supportive relationships give people ways to share experiences, ask for help, and feel cared for.

Name what you are looking for

You may want someone who listens, more regular contact, or support working through tension. Each points toward a different conversation or setting.

Start with a manageable connection

Consider reaching out to someone you already trust, or trying a group where conversation is organized around a shared interest.
