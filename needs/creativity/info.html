Understanding creativity

Creativity includes making things, expressing ideas, and exploring different ways of doing something.

Make room to experiment

Creative work can begin with adapting an idea, trying a material, or making a small draft. The first attempt can help you discover what you want to explore next.

Identify what would make it easier to begin

You might need tools, a workspace, feedback, or time alongside others making things. Start by naming the barrier you can address first.
